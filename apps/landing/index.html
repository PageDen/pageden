Features

Built for teams that plan with agents and keep the record

Give your agent a governed place to draft, your reviewers a durable place to comment, and your team a trusted record of what was decided.

🤖
Agent-drafted plans
Let Claude, Codex, or another MCP agent create and update planning documents inside a governed workspace.
💬
Anchored review comments
Reviewers can leave comments tied to the plan instead of losing feedback in chat threads.
📅
Version-safe revisions
Track every change, compare revisions, and keep agent edits from silently overwriting the wrong version.
✅
Canonical decisions
Promote accepted plans into the source of truth so agents and teammates know what to trust.

Obsidian to team truth
Bring Markdown thinking from Obsidian into a shared workspace when it needs team review.

Governed agent access

Draft

Your agent drafts the plan in Markdown

Ask an agent to turn context, constraints, and existing docs into a structured planning document instead of another disposable chat thread.

  • Agent-written Markdown plans
  • Workspace context and cited source material
  • Durable document, not temporary chat
Review

Your team reviews with comments attached to the source

A teammate or second agent can inspect the plan, leave anchored comments, and keep decisions connected to the document they affect.

  • Comments tied to the plan
  • Human + agent review loops
  • Decisions attached to the document
Canonical

Revise, audit, and promote the accepted plan

Resolve comments, inspect the revision history, and promote the accepted version into canonical team knowledge that agents and teammates can trust later.

  • Version history and activity trail
  • Conflict-aware or auditable updates
  • Canonical source-of-truth status
